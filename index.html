Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions From Buyers & Investors

What does an on-the-ground proxy service in Vietnam actually do?

A proxy service like ProxiGlobe acts as your physical representative inside Vietnam — inspecting factories, supervising container loading, verifying suppliers against official government registries, and attending trade shows on your behalf — so you get first-hand, photographed verification without booking a flight.

How much does a factory inspection or audit cost in Vietnam?

ProxiGlobe's published rates for field services (sample consolidation, container loading supervision, factory walkthroughs, entity verification) run roughly $120–$550 depending on scope, with emergency deployments at $400–$550. Exact pricing for each service is listed on this menu.

Can I set up a 100% foreign-owned company (WFOE) in Vietnam without traveling?

Yes. Our Legal Advisory Liaison service manages the full Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) process, plus the compliant bank account setup, in partnership with our licensed Vietnamese corporate law firm — handled on the ground while you stay wherever you are.

Are Western-template contracts enforceable in Vietnamese courts?

Generally, no. Vietnam requires contracts to align under local jurisdiction, and a dispute resolution clause through the Vietnam International Arbitration Centre (VIAC) is what makes a breach actually enforceable. ProxiGlobe's partner law firm drafts bilingual English/Vietnamese contracts built to hold up locally.

What happens if a factory stops responding mid-production?

That is exactly what our Emergency On-Site Factory Gate Drop service is built for: a representative is physically dispatched to the factory within hours to get face-to-face answers from management and report back with an objective, real-time status assessment.

Do I need a local lawyer to register a trademark in Vietnam?

Vietnam operates on a strict first-to-file basis — whoever files first owns the mark, regardless of use elsewhere. Registering directly with the National Office of Intellectual Property (NOIP) through a licensed local filer is the only way to prevent a supplier or competitor from filing your brand first.

What is the difference between a sourcing agent and a legal liaison?

A sourcing agent finds and vets suppliers and verifies physical operations. A legal liaison handles the contracts, entity registration, IP filings, and compliance that make those supplier relationships enforceable. ProxiGlobe is one of the few providers in Ho Chi Minh City offering both under a single accountable team.

Can profits be legally repatriated out of Vietnam?

Yes, but only through properly structured capital accounts and tax clearance certifications under State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) compliance. Our Cross-Border Profit Repatriation service structures the equity injection and withdrawal channels correctly from the start.
